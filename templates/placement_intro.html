How it works

  • Have pen and paper ready. Work every problem by hand and type only the final answer.
  • Each track starts in the middle. A right answer moves you up; a wrong one moves you down. That's about 3–6 questions per track and 40–60 minutes in total.
  • You won't see whether you were right. This keeps the test from turning into a lesson.
  • If you've never studied a topic, press Skip this track. If you don't know an answer, press I don't know. Honest answers give better homework.
  • You can stop at any time. Tracks you didn't reach keep their default "not assessed" state.
